# Code of Conduct

Codelit follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discriminatory conduct,
threats, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to mo@codelit.io. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards.

The full Contributor Covenant is available at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
